Replay a /record session recording in the terminal (space pauses, q quits)

USAGE
  $ omp play [FILE] [FLAGS]

ARGUMENTS
  FILE   Recording to play (default: the newest /record capture)

FLAGS
  -s, --speed=<value>       Playback speed multiplier (default: 1)
  -i, --idle-limit=<value>  Cap pauses between frames to this many seconds

EXAMPLES
  omp play
  omp play /tmp/omp-recordings/2026-09-22T10-00-00-1a2b3c4d.ompcast -s 2 -i 1
